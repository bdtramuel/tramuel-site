I quit y’all and your real life conversations about fake television.

Some folks become so defensive of things that they like, especially those absorbed through media more than the things that they actually do. That is not to say you should not or cannot enjoy reality television, I get it its entertaining. I have shows that I watch that I enjoy too. However, keep your reality television & real life separate being entertained is not the same as being engaged; be present and engaged in your own experiences.
