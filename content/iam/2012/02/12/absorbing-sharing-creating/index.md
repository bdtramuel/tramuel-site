There is an unmistakably dreamy and inspired side to me that is forever present, I express it by absorbing and sharing my appreciation for music and the arts. I enjoy creating too, however the desire to create poetry has eluded me.

While searching my email for a thought I typed myself, I found a little something I had written about ‘her’. I first believed it was something I found & liked enough to save but “*Today, hot tamales, the sunrise, the full moon, leather bags & scriptures*” is specific to only one.

Sometime early 2010 ...

*You don’t have to respond … this allows me to move on*

*Every day thoughts.*

*Every day images.*

*Every day sounds.*

*Every day reminders,*

*Time.*

*& still,*

*Today, I wish I would have made that trip.*

*Today, I look at your photo(s).*

*Today, I hear the sound of your voice.*

*Today, hot tamales, the sunrise, the full moon, leather bags & scriptures,*

*Time.*

*& still,*

*You don’t have to respond … this allows me to move on.*

I learned three things today ...

Love, whether the newness, the overflow or the loss of inspires you to create.

Poetry is about creating from what inspires you.

Time is a valuable asset, it renews.

I Am.
