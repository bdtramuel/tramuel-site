Last Sunday LMT’s mom called to ask if I wanted to join them in church [thirty minutes before service] to which I graciously declined.There has to be a reason I thought, however to ask “Why?” would have set up an hour long conversation. *“Just show me the baby!”* Moving skillfully through the battlefield of her mind I deflected, instead of “Why?” I stated …

![UBM](meezheadshot100x100-2_thumb.webp)

UBM

“Do you know what it means when a man goes to church with a woman?” [I’ve heard that before, I don’t know what it means but it worked.]

![MMD](m-dolphus-avatar_thumb.webp)

MMD

“Man, your son wants to join the church and I wanted to make sure you are there.”

![UBM](meezheadshot100x100-2_thumb1.webp)

UBM

“So you call thirty minutes prior?” [I didn’t say it but I thought it]

Silence

![MMD](m-dolphus-avatar_thumb1.webp)

MMD

“Well we can do it next weekend."

![UBM](meezheadshot100x100-2_thumb2.webp)

UBM

“Cool.”

![MMD](m-dolphus-avatar_thumb2.webp)

MMD

“I love you & I want to make passionate love to you.” [She didn’t say it but I’m sure she thought it]

I was excited; even though he is eight he understands the anchor in all of our lives, knowing God. My mother, her mother, the mission statement for the Y where he plays basketball, the oath for The Boys Scouts of America … there is a foundation constantly being poured into him, ready to build HIM up. Knowing my son I also couldn’t help but think there was something driving this decision. He is a Libra born during the week of the perfectionist. It is evident in how he approaches life, he is exacting, cool, charming with an abundant amount of love for his family; immediate and extended so I’m never amazed at anything he says or does.

I’m proud of him and I couldn’t wait to tell my mother. I hold off because I also know his mother and although she is a classically trained lyric soprano, she should have studied drama. Everything is a BIG production with her so there has to be a side story. The perfect opportunity for a follow up question arises when she calls for some sweet late night chit chat. I recently bought a new to me car so when *she* asks if *she* could ride it, naturally I assume *she* is referring to the car -\_- Eventually, we go for a spin and I ask how did LMT let her know he wanted to join the church to which she stated …

![Lil' Mr.T](lil-mr_thumb.webp)

Lil' Mr.T

“Mama, I’m ready to drink the grape juice now.”

I promise you that I’ve never laughed so hard in my life.

In gratitude to the Lord.

I Am.
