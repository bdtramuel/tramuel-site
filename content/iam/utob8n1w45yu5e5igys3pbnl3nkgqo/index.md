> That Mouf Only Take You So Far
>
> @rashidas.thomas

I laughed. Then I sat with it, and it stopped being funny in the way it started out funny.

We are the most talked-at people who have ever lived. Podcast, thread, carousel, keynote, a man in a rented studio explaining discipline to you at 6 a.m. I have a folder of saved posts I have never opened twice. I have screenshotted the same piece of advice from four different accounts, which means I did not need the advice. I needed something else.

That is the joke, and the joke is about me.

Nobody is waiting on more information. Everybody already knows. I know a woman who can explain attachment theory better than the people who named it and still sends the eleventh text. I know a man who kept a gym membership for years and called it community, which is what you call proximity when you need it to count for more. I have been that man. The knowledge was never the problem. The knowledge sat there, fully formed and completely correct and entirely outside of me.

The mouth can tell you you're worthy. It can say it beautifully. It can say it in a voice you would pay for.

But something has to get in.

A documentary can inspire you. A book can wake you up. A conversation with the right person at the wrong hour can crack something open. There is still a difference between being moved and being changed, and the difference is depth. How far in it went. Whether it reached the part of you that actually runs things, or only the part that enjoys hearing things.

That is what penetration is doing in that quote. It isn't the setup. It's the argument.

Here's the harder half. The thing has to be let in. Nobody gets penetrated with their arms folded. It asks you to be open, which is not a mood, it's a decision you make with your whole body. It asks you to trust something. It asks you to stop narrating for a minute.

Which might explain why so many of us live in the talking part. Processing. Debating. Naming our patterns with real fluency. The talking is safer. The talking never once asks you to be receptive. You can process for a decade and never move an inch off your position.

Overfed on words. Undernourished by experience.

The mouth can tell you to rest. Experience will cancel your Saturday and lay you flat before noon.

That mouf only take you so far.

I Remain
