# Change Take to Give

I'm constantly reminded of givers and takers; at work, at home, in these social and real streets.  I've written about it previously, ***[Givers Vs Takers](https://www.tramuel.com/iam/2011/10/13/givers-vs-takers/).***

Go read it, I'll wait.

I use to believe I was a giver but most of us fall into both categories at different times depending on the situation. In any relationship things are not always static, they evolve or devolve.

I tried to sum it up previously, however giving and taking are not even part of the equation. When you value your connections, your experiences should transcend into the flow of creating value in each other (& in yourself).

Logical calisthenics, change take to give.

I remain.
