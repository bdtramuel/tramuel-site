### **Relationship types -Emophilia and Romanticism**

**Emophilia** is a tendency to fall in love quickly and easily. The hypothesis of emophilia research is that people differ in the degree they need to be in love.

**Romanticism** is the intimate and pleasant feelings from the emotional desire of an individual for another person.

### **80/20 Theory**

The 80/20 relationship theory states that you can only get about 80% of your wants and needs from a healthy relationship, while the remaining 20% you need to provide for yourself.
