They said heal. I'm healing.

They said let go. I'm working on it.

But I’m still triple-checking your grammar, color-coding my calendar, and giving laser beam side-eyes when your chaos tries to flirt with my peace.

Healing didn’t erase my Virgo tendencies. It just gave them purpose.

Now, instead of overthinking your text at 2 AM? I overthink my boundaries and then communicate them clearly, possibly with bullet points.

Instead of fixing broken people? I fix my credit, my posture, my plate, and occasionally your resume.

You’re welcome.

Yes, I still stack my dishes like Tetris. Yes, I still clean to relax. No, I will not apologize for organizing my thoughts in Apple Notes, a physical journal, and a spreadsheet.

Virgo season is coming. And this time? It’s emotionally intelligent, the evolved kind.

*Evil Voice Laugh*

Brian,

Professional overthinker, semi-professional pizza enthusiast.
