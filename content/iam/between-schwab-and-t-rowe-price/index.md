Co-Star lives on my home screen, in a widget next to Schwab and T. Rowe Price. This morning it said, “*Be your own dream.”*

I daydream a lot. Not the sleeping kind, where things drift wherever they want to go. Mine are more like events. Things I’d like to happen. A conversation going a certain way. A place I can see myself living.

Some of them return often enough that I’ve stopped treating them as idle.

The app had more to say. *Stop waiting for permission to want what you want.* Then it told me the person holding me back was me, making excuses and calling them wisdom.

I read that twice.

I’m good at the excuses. I can explain why something won’t work before I’ve finished wanting it. I’ve called that being realistic, or practical, for a long time. Some of the time it was.

A daydream doesn’t promise anything. Most of mine won’t happen the way I picture them. Some won’t happen at all. I’d still rather see them all the way through than talk myself out of them halfway.

The daydream is one thing. What I do with it is another.
