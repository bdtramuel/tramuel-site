Your Daily Vibe, The Pattern (Astrological App) Jan 29 2023

*You're incredibly mentally proficient, but at times you may have trouble turning off your brain. To slow things down, try engaging all of your senses. This can divert some of your focus - so vou can take a break from constantly processing.*

I fell asleep on the left, but woke without center. Five-hundred count thread softens me, only some unwrap before sunrise.

The Sun is shining but it’s cold.

*“Alexa? What’s the temperature?”*

I’ve come to my faltering senses lately. In a moment of clarity arriving maybe minutes too late.

Watching the sun beat in through the windows, creating dramatic light and shadow…

Now the strong scent of coffee, w/ the mild scent of honey—

Gifts, of taste, sweetness, refreshment and surprise.
