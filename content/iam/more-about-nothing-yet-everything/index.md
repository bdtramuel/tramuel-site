**Olá**

Got a DM poking me to see if I was real. Apparently my profile picture looks AI-generated.

So I posted the whole photo. AI would have evened out the beard. Real boy, 56, all my own teeth. It was taken at Camp North End before it became what it is now. Zoom in on the sunglasses, and you can see my car door open and part of the parking lot.

The first reply was a bot. It said “Olá.”

That same week, Amex updated its terms. If I let an AI into my account, whatever it does is on me. I read the whole thing to make sure I didn’t owe them my third grandbaby.

I don’t.

**Marked Free**

The car stays parked these days. Anything past three miles, I take an Uber. Tuesday that meant the rainbow trout at the bar at 131 Main, then waiting out the surge before I rode home.

I also put a Charlotte FC playoff match on my calendar. I’m not going. I live a few blocks from the stadium, and I need to know which Saturday afternoon I’m not going anywhere either.

The calendar has it marked free.

**Triple10**

At 3 AM I followed a tip from a stranger’s post: type “Thanksgiving feast for 10” into the Walmart app and use code DOUBLE10 at checkout. It had worked for others. For me, it was TRIPLE10. Twenty-six items, turkey included, for $25.

Glad I ordered early. The Panthers were home that afternoon, and no driver was getting to my building.

I’m donating all of it.

Most of my 3 AM purchases are books and shoes I don’t need. I’d ordered three books that same week.

I might have to lock my cards and only buy turkeys for other people.
