**Training What Can’t Be Seen**

I’ve been thinking about transformation. Not the kind that happens overnight, but the kind you train for. The internal reps. The mental stretches. The soul shifts.

Logical Calisthenics, something I’ve leaned on for what feels like a decade, offers a way to reflect on how transformation is showing up in my life right now.

It’s about flexing thought into action. Turning fixed mindsets into fluid movement.

Each post is a short set, a warmup for the mind and spirit, grounded in a single shift:

From *hate to love*

From *fall to rise*

From *take to give*

From *lose to find*

From *won’t to will*

These aren’t motivational quotes. They’re personal practices. I’m not offering answers just the rhythm I’ve found in the questions. I write these as someone still in the gym of becoming.

Suit up. Breathe deep. Let’s move,

I Am
