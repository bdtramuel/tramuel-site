[![Raven's Brew](https://photo.goodreads.com/books/1266527400m/6128439.jpg)](http://www.goodreads.com/book/show/6128439-raven-s-brew)[Raven's Brew](http://www.goodreads.com/book/show/6128439-raven-s-brew) by[Christina P. Davis](http://www.goodreads.com/author/show/2795193.Christina_P_Davis "Christina P. Davis")
My rating: [5 of 5 stars](http://www.goodreads.com/review/show/163760713)

My copy arrived this morning. I skimmed through the contents to get a feel for the book. The titles are creative and grabbed my attention, the actual poem(s) held it. I began reading on the ride to work (Mass Transit - Never read & drive),while at lunch, break and on the ride home. I was unable to stop reading, I read each once, twice and thrice. First to gain an understanding of the writers intentions, second to gain understanding of my own interpretation and lastly to relate that understanding to my personal experiences. I love poetry and appreciate the way writers effectively communicate by colliding words through thought onto paper. This is a book review, so Raven's Brew inspires creative thought through a series serendipitous occurrences ... listen to your {He}art ~ ![Red heart](wlemoticon-redheart.webp)
