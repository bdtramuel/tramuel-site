What started out for me as a creative way to celebrate my birthday with my family & fam’ [Immediate & Extended] turned into an awe inspiring display of; intuition, sensation, feeling & thought. Each representing a season. I asked everyone to share their thoughts on age, anything that they were willing to discuss. Initially it was going to be one post but the responses I’ve received all deserve a separate post. I will share one everyday for the month of September.

My mother responded first, so she is up first. I read her entry over and over and over again, she speaks with so much love that my heart was full today. Today was a good day.
