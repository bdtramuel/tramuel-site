# Fantasy Vs. Perspective

![](pawan-sharma-364696-unsplash.webp)

## Fantasy

Creative imagination, unrestrained fancy. A capricious or fantastic idea; a conceit. An imagined event or sequence of mental images; a daydream fulfilling a wish or psychological need. Obsolete, a hallucination.

## Perspective

A representation. The appearance of objects in depth as perceived by normal binocular vision. An accurate point of view and the ability to see it, objectively. With or without a correct or sensible understanding of something’s true importance.

There is one existence where fantasy and perspective collide for me; sometimes when I am being silent, I fantasize about being engulfed in silence.

Real.
