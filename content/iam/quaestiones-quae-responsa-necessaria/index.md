### **Unum**

*How do you sever connections so fast and seemingly never look back?*

There is nothing wrong with living souls, regardless of gender, having few connections, lacking long generational ties, or seeking new ones. For many, the desire for connection stems not from a genuine desire to share but to fulfill their emotional voids.

We should prioritize the quality and depth of our relationships, particularly those with ourselves, over the sheer quantity of connections we maintain with others, expecting them to fill emotional voids.

### **Duo**

*What is it about her?*

While not an exhaustive list of personality traits, these are the ways love shows up, in line with what I need.

Supports my independence and respects my boundaries.

Opens my mind to what having it all would look like.

Never limit me to one choice or the one she would make.

Is always there when I need her.

Has my back no matter what.

Challenges me without insulting or demeaning me.

Helps me search for the lessons in my setbacks and encourages me to keep trying.

Believes in me.

Makes me want to be a better person.

Respects me, my experiences and my mind.

Values me as I am. my experiences and my mind.

She wants to serve me, not that I Am a mighty King, but as a way to enhance my life and because I reciprocate in a way that enhances hers.

I am hopeful for the emotion, the abstraction that is hard to quantify and define…

*Love*
