“***It is the character of very few Wo/Men to honor without envy a friend who has prospered”***

I’ve been holding court with The Key and she has been trying to reconcile envy and jealousy in her relationship categories: Love, Work, Family and Friendships.

She said, and I quote

***“A win for others is not a loss (for you)”***

There are two primary emotional states when considering this:

### **Jealousy**

Jealousy refers to negative thoughts and feelings that incorporate fear, insecurity, discontent and worry over the perceived talents, property and/or advantages of another person with the threat that the person can replace them. Jealousy is an emotion that is characterized by overwhelming insecurity. However, it is rarely expressed in any single behavior.

### **Envy**

Envy is often used interchangeably with the word jealousy, since some people are jealous and envious at the same time, but as an emotion its definition differs. Envy occurs within a person when they specifically lack another person's talents, property and/or advantages and specifically desire them for themselves, so much so that they will wish ill will on the person. Envy is characterized by overwhelming hate and self-importance.

I imagine if confronted, a *“friend”* might respond that they are genuinely happy, proud, excited for you and then they hit you with the BIG BUT, proclaiming it is neither jealousy or envy and will now push a narrative of disapproval or dissent.
