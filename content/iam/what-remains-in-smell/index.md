A scent can undo us. The trace of their perfume on a scarf, the way the kitchen shifts when they cook, even the faintest whiff of a flower they once mentioned in passing. Smell carries presence into absence, and suddenly the distance is gone.

What remains in smell is the nearness of love; unseen, untouchable, but undeniable.

— Brian
