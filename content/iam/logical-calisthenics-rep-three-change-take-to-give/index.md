**Aligned in Offering**

Taking is survival. Giving is evolution.

When I stopped asking, “What can I get from this?” and started asking, “What can I offer?” the math of my life changed. Giving didn’t drain me; it rooted me. Deepened me. Aligned me.

Giving is a discipline. It’s not charity, it’s clarity. It doesn’t mean yes to everything. It means yes with intention. A gift of energy, time, presence, truth.

This rep is about letting generosity become muscle memory,
