Intimate. Relating to one's deepest nature. Essential; innermost. Marked by informality and privacy. Deeply personal, private, or secret. Belonging to a thing by its very nature. Marked by close acquaintance, association, or familiarity.

Personal. Of or relating to a particular person; private. Of the body. Belonging to a particular person and no one else.

Intimate is defined, in part, as deeply personal. Personal isn't defined as intimate. One word holds the other. Not the other way around.

Personal is about whose it is. It belongs to one person and no one else, which also makes that person the one who answers for it. Accountability is important, and a wise choice to make.

Intimate is about how close you have to get to see it. It's the innermost part, what belongs to me by nature instead of by ownership. And the definition keeps bringing someone else into it. Close acquaintance. Familiarity. Somebody has to be near.

These two words came out of a romance ending, and out of the intimacy that kept coming afterward from friends and family.

I've been writing here since 2008. Most of what ends up on this page is personal. The intimate parts stay with the people they belong to, so some of this gets parentheses.

What I noticed is how easily the pieces come apart. Sometimes it's the ( ) without any of the relationship. Sometimes it's the relationship without the ( ).

The first is easy. Nobody has to do the work of getting to know someone new. Nobody's uneasy with you there late at night or still there in the morning. The romantic piece is done.

The second is closeness without the body. A man and a woman near each other, with the ( ) set aside.

Either one can soothe, cover, placate, or satisfy the need. All while proving to yourself you can stand on whatever line you've drawn.

I've been in both. I liked being the one who made it easy. Needed, or wanted, or used. I'm still not sure which word is right. I get satisfaction from satisfying someone else.

But I know now what I want.

I don't want you to belong to me. I want you to be completely free and still choose me every day.

Choose wisely and speak only what is needed.
