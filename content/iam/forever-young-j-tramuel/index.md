# A Coming of The Age

Youth? What youth?

We have forced our children to grow up too soon

We expose them to adult experiences and wonder where things go wrong

You might not think it’s that serious but it’s more serious than you think

Be cognizant of how you walk, talk, react, and interact around young people

Live up to their expectations

Recognize that there is a separation

Kids are not adults and adults are not kids...

Joi is my family and the voice behind [The Society for Hip Hop & Education](http://hiphopeducationsociety.weebly.com/), an organization upholding the TRUE image of #HipHop and advocating for community empowerment.
