What remains…

A heart that is somehow is still hopeful, imperfectly mended, and ready for being loved properly,

A sense of resilience that life isn’t what happens to you, but what you have conquered,

The value in saying ‘no’ because I said so, no explanation needed,

The security of knowing that God has me, even when I don’t have me,

Peace, because it’s the healthiest option: emotionally, mentally, physically, and spiritually,

What I accepted before will happily be declined,

Knowledge of knowing where I have come from and where I am, and where I am going,

What remains is Unreservedly Me!

*— Shared by Sarith*
