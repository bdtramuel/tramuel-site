Love changes shape. What begins as fire softens into warmth, what feels like certainty learns to bend, what’s captivating in the beginning grows quiet but deeper over time. Love is not fixed, it shifts, stretches, and reshapes itself to meet the people we become. What remains is not the spark alone, but the steady glow, the willingness to keep choosing each other as everything else changes.

— Brian
