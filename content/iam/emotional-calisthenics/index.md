First, they love you, then they hate you, then they love you again.

Then they spin the block, but always on some passive-agress-her terms masked as humor.

Received a text message at one-thirty ante meridiem— A meme using the guy tapping his finger on his head, having just thought of something. He’s figured out a way to avoid his problems, and is passing it along to you as a genius strategy…

I delayed my signal reaction, not allowing my amygdala to get hijacked. The meme was followed by—

***“This you?”***

***“Lol”***

***“I can’t sleep. I read your little post.”***

Deep, deep, deep, deep, deep, deep, deep, deep, deep, deep, deep sigh.

[***Love, Undeserving Of***](https://www.tramuel.com/iam/love-undeserving-of/)is the post referenced with the following highlighted…

***“At this point, I’m now engaged in emotional calisthenics and counterweights have been attached to all of my feelings.”***

I’m not sure why this elicited a reaction, maybe for the literary merit ;) Although, there is truth in the meme, I won’t play victim and I accept my role in how things ended. However, I only had a cameo appearance, I was not a full cast member.

Don’t let pride make you hide the love,

City Boys Up
