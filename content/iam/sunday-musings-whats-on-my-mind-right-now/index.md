# Of Carbohydrates,

## In the case of health...

I wish my Nike Running App could talk to the treadmill. Speaking of running on the treadmill, I hate it but I found a way to enjoy it. I focus on the equipment in front of me; foot strikes, breathing ... listen, I believe I just taught myself to meditate while on a treadmill.

> "I can only meditate when I am walking. When I stop I cease to think; my mind only works with my legs." - Jean Jacques Rousseau

 I'm also learning to encourage health through self-understanding by continuing to practice non-allness thinking. We are all absolutely unique and nothing is one size fits all concerning our health.
