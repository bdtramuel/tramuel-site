**Envy.**

Most of us use it interchangeably with jealousy. I suppose this is because some are jealous and envious at the same time. However, their definitions differ when we consider envy as an emotion. I witness it frequently while working. Today a peer was pulled from our normal process and given a separate project. The shade as it was announced was epic. When someone lacks another person’s talents, skill or advantages and clearly wants them for themselves ...  witness eyes turn green.

*“It is the character of very few to honor without envy a friend who has prospered.”*
