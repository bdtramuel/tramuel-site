Morning light through the window.

The streets outside are quiet. Different from the weekend energy of New Orleans. I'm staying in the CBD, and after a few days of moving around, the quiet feels good.

I'm not starving, but I go downstairs anyway. Familiar. Free. The Fountain Lounge.

Four days here and I've tried three different things from the menu. I still haven't touched the buffet.

Today, it's light. A breakfast sandwich. Breakfast potatoes. Orange juice.

The service has been consistent. Pleasant without being performative. The food has been fresh and flavorful. Nothing complicated. Just good.

The coffee, though, hasn't quite gotten there.

So I walk to French Truck for chicory coffee. Hot. For here. In a ceramic mug.

Apparently, iced is the popular choice to go.

I sit in the big picture window and watch people move through the morning. More light. Canal Street. The streetcar line. The city waking up.

I'll head back to the hotel soon and spend some time by the pool.

Not in it. Fresh ink.

Worth it.

A book. Some journaling. A little time doing nothing in particular.

And today, I keep noticing something else.

The texts. The calls. The gifts. The friends who have found their own way to help me celebrate.

Today is my birthday.

And I am loved.

There's still tomorrow to look forward to. Dinner at Dakar NOLA.

But today doesn't need much more than this.

Morning light.

Quiet streets.

Chicory coffee in a ceramic mug.

A book.

Fresh ink.

And the very good feeling of being remembered,

I Am.
