The first version of this page said I was always interesting and usually funny. I'd settle for usually on both. The part about being nit-picky held up.

I'm Brian TramueL. Born in Roanoke, Charlotte since 1998, give or take. Professional overthinker, semi-professional pizza enthusiast.

I started writing here in 2008. Early on it was mostly opinions: dating, music, friendship, and numbered lists of things other people should stop doing. I posted 136 times in 2011, which tells you something about 2011.

It's quieter now. These days a post is more likely to be about a coffee, a $100 bike with a good saddle, a watch with a small face, or a city I went to by myself.

Sensory Inputs is what I noticed on a given day. More About Nothing, Yet Everything is the lighter stuff. Eyetramuel is the photographs.

I don't write for circumstances, but solace from them.
